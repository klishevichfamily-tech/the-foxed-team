The Foxed Team

A survival server for builders, engineers, and explorers. Build factories and flying ships, establish nations, trade with other players, and shape the world together.

Opening December 1, 2026

The project is currently in beta, and the modpack is still being prepared. Everyone will be welcome to join when the server opens, without an application.

Server features

  • Factories, mechanisms, and automation with Create.
  • Flying ships and aerial battles with Create Aeronautics.
  • Player-built cities, nations, alliances, and wars.
  • Floating islands, unusual biomes, and continents to explore.
  • A diamond-based economy and trade between players.
  • BedWars with Create Aeronautics blocks, assemblers, and flying contraptions.

Build your own future

Start by gathering resources and building your first shelter. Develop your technology, automate production, and work toward your own factory and flying ship.

Play independently or team up with friends. Run a small workshop, build a city, or help your nation develop an entire industrial network. Your settlements, alliances, and conflicts will write the world's history.

Community and fair play

No world wipes are planned, and donations will offer cosmetic rewards only. Griefing is prohibited outside official wars; the administration will investigate reports. During official wars, enemy buildings and ships may be destroyed under the server's rules.

Stay in touch through text chat and voice chat groups. Building competitions, aerial battles, and BedWars tournaments are planned.

How to join

Minecraft version: 1.21.1. Modpack installation instructions and the server address will be posted here when preparations for opening are complete.
